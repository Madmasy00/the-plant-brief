Issue 001: Plants as biochemical engineers

Read the articles ↓

How plants organise their internal machinery, communicate through chemistry, recruit microbial partners, and adapt to environmental challenges.

Concept of the week

Root exudates: the chemistry beneath our feet

Roots release sugars, amino acids, organic acids and specialised metabolites into the rhizosphere. These compounds can mobilise nutrients, feed microorganisms or alter microbial behaviour. But a crucial scientific question remains: when microbes respond, are they detecting a signal—or simply consuming a resource?

Think about it: Could we breed crops that recruit beneficial microbes more reliably, reducing dependence on fertilisers?
